USDA Conservation Reserve Program

National grassland ecosystem modeling

Collaborated on Random Forest models of forb and grass cover using Sentinel-1 synthetic aperture radar (SAR), optical imagery, climate, and field-survey data; led spectral biodiversity modeling.

Owned Google Earth Engine coding, image processing, and code infrastructure. Vegetation outputs supported the team’s broader ecosystem-service models; the later CONUS expansion remains unpublished.

USDA Conservation Reserve Program

Mississippi CRP forest assessment

Inherited and improved existing Mississippi CRP models in R. By incorporating GEDI-derived canopy-height data into an existing Sentinel-1/2 Random Forest biomass model, reduced biomass prediction error (RMSE) by approximately 12% compared with the original model.

Used Forest Inventory and Analysis (FIA) measurements for training and validation of forest-attribute models in a USDA pilot assessing bottomland hardwood forests. The canopy-height product combined GEDI LiDAR observations with Landsat imagery; improvements varied by forest attribute.

Southern Puget Sound

Forest resource inventory for the Squaxin Island Tribe

Applied existing remote-sensing-derived products to support a Tribal forest inventory and stewardship of reacquired ancestral homelands in southern Puget Sound.

Applied LCMS land-cover and disturbance data, LEMMA forest-condition metrics, and Meta canopy-height estimates to assess forest age, structure, and biomass. Integrated these products with local management insights to inform land acquisition, stewardship, and harvest planning as partners balanced old-growth protection with potential timber income.

Master’s research

Forest conservation in sustainable palm oil

Built a novel spatial dataset from more than 100 Roundtable on Sustainable Palm Oil (RSPO) planning reports to evaluate voluntary, market-driven sustainability certification in Indonesian oil-palm landscapes.

Georeferenced and digitized plantation and High Conservation Value boundaries, reconstructed development histories, derived annual forest-change metrics in Google Earth Engine, and used inverse probability weighting, difference-in-differences, and Cox survival models in R to evaluate forest conservation.
